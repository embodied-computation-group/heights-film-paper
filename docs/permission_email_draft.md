**Subject:** Permission to share your Dubai #6 video as open research material

Hi Sasha,

I'm a neuroscientist at Aarhus University in Denmark, and I'm writing to ask your permission to share your video
"Most Crazy Girl Ever Viki Odintcova. Dubai #6" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDVsGyhteEg) as part of the openly
available stimulus materials of our research, so that other scientists can use it in their own studies.

I should say first that we have already used it. We found it on YouTube and have been using it, muted, as a film that
people watch during experiments. In one study, about 330 people had their brains scanned while they watched it. In
another, about 150 people watched it online and rated, second by second, how intense it felt and how pleasant or
unpleasant it was. Very few films produce such a strong and consistent reaction: how intense it feels climbs steadily
as you go up the tower, peaks on the roof, and drops again at the end, and people's bodily anxiety rises clearly by
the time it finishes. Your camera work is the reason it works so well.

We are now publishing these studies, and I've attached a draft of the first paper and its supplement. Our request is
that we may:

- include the video file in the paper's open research archive (for example Zenodo), where researchers can download it
  together with our ratings;
- show still frames from it in the paper and its materials (Supplementary Figure S1 and screenshots of our rating
  task);
- always with full credit to you and the team (@olegcricket, @a_mavrin, @viki_odintcova) and links to your YouTube
  and Instagram, and for non-commercial research and teaching only.

Brain-imaging datasets are reused by many labs for years, so the film could become a standard tool for studying fear
and anxiety, and every study that uses it would credit and link back to your channel.

If you agree, a short reply saying so is all we need. If you would like any conditions attached, just let me know.
I'm happy to send you the final papers when they are published.

Many thanks, and congratulations on a remarkable film.
