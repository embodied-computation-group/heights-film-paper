## Open access (checked 2026-10-04)

- **Status (Micah, from ChatGPT's research, 2026-10-04):** Cognition and Emotion is covered by the Danish T&F
  agreement, but this year's national quota is used up. Free OA therefore depends on acceptance falling in a year
  with quota left (and other factors). Green OA is allowed either way. Plan: green OA unless the agreement applies.

- **Danish agreement:** Royal Danish Library has a 2024-2028 agreement with Taylor & Francis on behalf of Danish
  universities and research institutions. It covers immediate open access (Creative Commons licence) in T&F's
  **Open Select hybrid journals**, for **corresponding authors** employed at covered institutions. Fully gold OA
  journals are excluded. There is a **national annual cap** on the number of articles (number not stated on the page).
  Source: https://pro.kb.dk/en/licensing/open-access-licence-agreements/taylor-francis
  - Search summaries (not the KB page itself) say authors get a 15% APC discount once the cap is reached; not
    confirmed on the primary page.
- **Not yet confirmed:** that Cognition and Emotion is in the Open Select list, and that Aarhus University is a covered
  institution. Check in T&F's Journal Finder / the list of Open Select journals, or ask the AU library. The cap may be
  used up late in the year, so the timing of acceptance can matter.
- **Green OA fallback:** T&F allows the Accepted Manuscript on a personal website at any time after publication; in
  institutional/subject repositories after an embargo of typically 12 months (science, technology, medicine) or
  18 months (social sciences and humanities) from publication of the Version of Record. Which embargo applies to
  Cognition and Emotion is not stated on the general page. Recommended acknowledgement: "This is an Accepted Manuscript
  of an article published by Taylor & Francis in [JOURNAL TITLE] on [date of publication], available at:
  https://doi.org/[Article DOI]." No rights-retention / zero-embargo option is mentioned on that page.
  Source: https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/research-impact/sharing-versions-of-journal-articles/
- A preprint (e.g. PsyArXiv) before submission is a separate question; check the journal's preprint policy in the
  instructions for authors.

## Instructions for authors (Cognition and Emotion, updated 10 June 2026)

Extracted from the journal's author instructions by Micah (2026-10-04); not re-checked here (the page blocks automated
fetching).

**Article type and length**
- Papers reporting one experiment should typically be **Brief Articles**. The confirmatory and exploratory samples
  are one experiment (same procedure; exploratory sample used to fix the plan and as the H3 reference).
- **Brief Article:** max 4,000 words *including* abstract, references and footnotes (stricter of two wordings used);
  max **two tables/figures combined**; max **25 references**; unstructured abstract 200 words; 1-5 keywords.
- Full Article: 8,000-10,000 words including abstract, references, footnotes; abstract 200 words; 1-5 keywords.
  Do not pad.
- **Decision (2026-10-04): Brief Article.** Display 1 = composite figure (STICSA + time series, Micah); display 2 =
  table of the registered tests. Everything else goes to the supplement (Figshare).

**Format and files**
- Initial submission format-free; consistent layout and reference style; figures/tables may be embedded. Consistent
  UK or US English (we use UK English).
- Section order: title page; abstract; keywords; Introduction; Materials and Methods; Results; Discussion;
  acknowledgments; disclosure of interest; references; appendices; tables with captions; figures; figure captions.
- Title page: full author names, affiliations, corresponding author email, ORCIDs. Review is single blind.
- LaTeX accepted: compiled PDF + ZIP of sources including .bib. Editable manuscript at revision; editable tables.

**Statements**
- Funding (grant numbers, or no funding); Disclosure of interest; Generative AI declaration (actual use; do not claim
  none); Data availability (URL/DOI or truthful restrictions); ethics and informed consent in Methods.
- Ethics: an explanation is allowed where approval is not required for non-interventional studies; do not assume the
  film induction qualifies. The basis must be confirmed by the author; never invent approval or an exemption.
- CRediT contributions, for author confirmation. Data sharing encouraged (Basic Data Sharing Policy). Verified
  preregistration, data, code and materials links where available.

**Figures, supplements, costs**
- Figures readable at review; production: 1,200 dpi line art, 600 dpi greyscale, 300 dpi colour. Tables understandable
  on their own.
- Supplementary files via Figshare. Permission needed for reproduced third-party material, including film stills (our
  figures contain no film stills).
- No submission/publication/page fees; online colour free, print colour extra. Plan: subscription publication + green
  OA; keep the accepted manuscript. These instructions do not state the repository embargo.
