## Context (news coverage)

The film shows the December 2016 rooftop shoot at Cayan Tower, Dubai, with model Viki Odintcova and filmmaker
Alexander (Sasha) Tikhomirov. News reports describe a separate full video of the photo shoot by Mavrin Studios,
published on YouTube in late January 2017; our film is Tikhomirov's own vlog of the trip (uploaded 22 February 2017). The stunt
was done without permission; the building owner (Cayan Group) condemned it and announced legal action, and Dubai
police summoned the model. Sources: HuffPost (2017-02-21,
https://www.huffpost.com/entry/viki-odintkova-dubai-building_n_58aca26fe4b06e5f777b71a0); Al Arabiya
(2017-02-19, https://english.alarabiya.net/variety/2017/02/19/Dubai-police-summon-Russian-skyscraper-stunt-model);
Gulf News (https://gulfnews.com/uae/models-death-defying-stunt-in-dubai-goes-viral-1.1979532).
